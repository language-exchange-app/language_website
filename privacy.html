Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to TalkiDate ("we," "our," or "us"). We are fundamentally committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy comprehensively details how we collect, utilize, disclose, and securely safeguard your personal information when you access or use our mobile language exchange application (the "App") and its associated ecosystems.

Please review this Privacy Policy with care. If you do not explicitly agree with the frameworks and practices outlined herein, please discontinue access and use of the App immediately.

1. Information We Collect

In order to provide a functional and collaborative environment, we collect information across three main vectors: information you actively provide, data recorded automatically through use, and information derived from trusted third parties.

Information You Provide Directly to Us

  • Account Data: During registration, we collect foundational credentials including your name, email address, age, native language(s), and target language(s) you intend to learn.
  • Profile Information: To enhance community matching, you may optionally furnish expanded public metadata such as a profile picture, gender identity, personal interests, and specific language acquisition milestones.
  • Communication Content: We securely process and store messages, photos, audio dispatches, and active metadata generated during real-time text, voice, or video interactions within the App to facilitate seamless communication between you and your exchange partners.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Analytics: We maintain records of interactive sessions within the App, factoring in timestamp logs, feature utilization frequencies, duration metrics, and peer-to-peer engagement tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information harvested is systematically applied to cultivate, monitor, and optimize our worldwide language exchange network. Primary execution vectors include:

  • Configuring, operationalizing, and maintaining your personal profile and account ecosystem.
  • Algorithmatically matching you with complementary native speakers based on language priorities, and personal interests.
  • Hosting and preserving structural safety over internal text, voice, and video streaming operations.
  • Evaluating engagement metrics to design intuitive software optimizations and fix systemic errors.
  • Defending community integrity through advanced moderation tools targeted at detecting fraud, platform harassment, or explicit policy violations.
  • Broadcasting essential software notifications, terms modifications, security alerts, and customer service follow-ups.

3. Sharing Your Information

We hold a strict policy against the sale of user personal data. Information pathways are only shared transparently under the following explicit criteria:

  • With Peer Users: Public profile modules (such as your display name, chosen languages, bio, and visual avatar) are openly searchable to foster prospective matching. Private dialogue text or files are strictly confined to the explicit recipients involved.
  • With Verified Service Providers: We partner with established third-party processors to execute foundational services like managed cloud hosting infrastructure, real-time messaging pipelines, analytics engines, and system support ticketing. All processors are bound by rigid confidentiality agreements.
  • For Legal Protection & Compliance: We reserve the right to share information if strictly mandated by binding regulatory laws, subpoenas, judicial court orders, or if deemed fundamentally vital to shield the immediate physical safety, rights, or property of our users, staff, or public interest.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We strictly store your personalized datasets for the duration your account remains active or as required to successfully fulfill core application operations.

  • Account Deletion: Users can execute an absolute account and data purge by submitting a formal request via email.
  • Processing Window: Following a validated deletion request, all personal linkages will be irreversibly wiped or completely anonymized from active operational clusters within thirty (30) days, except where legal compliance archives mandate longer tracking.

5. International Data Transfers

Your collected information may be transferred to, and processed upon, cloud server infrastructure situated outside of your domestic territory, state, or country, where local data privacy statutes may differ. If you interface with the platform from outside Venezuela, kindly notice that data sets are securely routed to and processed within Europe.

6. Information Security Standards

We deploy robust organizational and multi-layer technical barriers engineered to prevent unauthorized visibility, data leaking, arbitrary altering, or loss of information. While we enforce industry-standard defensive controls, no digital routing protocol or database mechanism can be guaranteed as completely impervious to malicious breach.

7. Children's Privacy Mandates

Our platform operations are structurally restricted from serving individuals under the age of 18. We do not purposefully target or aggregate information from children. If it is brought to our attention that data from a minor has been gathered without verified parental or legal guardian authorization, immediate steps will be executed to purge the data from our networks completely.

8. Amendments to This Policy

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy periodically. We will post notification of modifications inside the application framework and refresh the "Last Updated" field at the top of this document. Continual utilization of the service past modification announcements implies acceptance of the updated practices.
